When 100% Is Not 100%: A Statistical Dilemma in Rare-Disease Meta-Analysis

How can all reported patients respond to treatment while a pooled meta-analytic estimate suggests a smaller response proportion? This planned article will examine why extreme proportions challenge conventional meta-analysis, how continuity corrections affect small studies, and why the choice of method matters for interpretation.
